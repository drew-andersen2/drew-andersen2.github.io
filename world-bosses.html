Azmodan, Lord of Sin

Shipped the first new world boss since the launch of Diablo 4. We successfully imported the Azmodan rig and some existing animations from Diablo 3 in order to give him new life in D4. While we pay homage to the Diablo 3 version of Azmodan, I worked to bring the quality of the fight up to the standards of a Diablo 4 encounter.

The Hollow Seraph

A corrupted celestial entity that forces players to manage light/dark polarity while avoiding sweeping wing attacks.
